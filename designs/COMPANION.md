# A companion: someone who walks with you

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14. Written for
[PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md): your companion stays with your character across deaths.

The review's gap: there are no companions, only the opening's walking contact and escorts. A
companion is **one person who chose to walk with you**, with his own opinions, his own limit, and a
life that can end.

## Who they are

One at most per character. He comes from the world, never a menu:

| Where from | How |
|---|---|
| Someone you saved | Survivors Trapped, a distress call, a hostage freed: at the end, if you were kind and he has nowhere to go, he asks to come |
| A wanderer | a wanderer who knows you well (regard high, 3 or more meetings) asks once |
| The opening's contact | after the opening ([DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-fixer)), if you stood with his street against Ferda |
| A faction's lad | Known with a faction, its quartermaster can send one of his people with you for a real day (a loan, not a friend) |

Each has a background of his own (a mechanic, a medic, a hunter, a soldier, a nobody), a voice
(WRITING.md, "The voices") and three things he cares about.

## How he plays

- **Walks with you**: the opening's walking contact's following and the escort block's pace,
  waits when told, keeps behind you in a fight, uses the base game's AI to shoot. A body only near
  you; when you log off he waits at your camp as data.
- **Does one thing well**: by his background, one help a day (patches you, reads a track, hotwires,
  knows a price).
- **Talks**: a line at things that matter to him (a door mark, a body, the line, a choice you made),
  never more than one every few minutes. You can talk to him at any time (the talk screen).
- **Approval**: every choice he sees moves his regard, by what he cares about (*a Haven man* likes
  you sharing food and hates you selling people; *a Red Hand lad* likes a clean deal and hates
  waste). Shown as a word on the Standing page (*"Trusts you"*, *"Uneasy"*).
- **A limit**: past his breaking point (regard low, or the one thing he won't stand for: a
  child-safe house sold, a bitten man shot unasked), he leaves, says why, and goes home. He's
  remembered as gone, not dead.
- **Can die for good**: killed, he's dead for the season. His name goes on the wall, and the camp
  he came from says so.
- **Remembered**: what happened to him is a line in the season's story and the epilogue's names.

## Caps

One per character. He never carries loot for you beyond what he wears, never revives you, never
makes you stronger than one more rifle and one help a day. Factions treat him as your people: what he
does counts as yours.

## Order of work

1. One source (someone you saved), following, waiting, dying for good, as data offline.
2. Talk, the help a day, approval and the limit.
3. The other sources; the season's story and the wall.

## Settled with the author (2026-11-14)

- A companion of another faction never walks into a camp that hates his faction: he waits outside, at a
  spot in sight of the gate, and says why once.
