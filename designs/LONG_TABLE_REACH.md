# The Long Table's reach

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14 ("All of them"). The Long Table
([DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-long-table)) is one family of four to seven in one farmhouse,
seen only within 300 m of the farm and by the host on the road. The author wants them far more
present. They stay a family, not a faction: no reputation, never a player taken, bodies only near
players. Their reach is mostly sound, props, voices and rumour.

## What grows

| # | Idea | What happens | Reach |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every season** | The season's chance goes from 0.75 to 1: there's a house every season unless the map has no place for one | data |
| 2 | **Supper signs** | A board at road junctions within 2 km of the farm: "SUPPER. ALL WELCOME." and an arrow toward the farm. Put out near players only; they move with the house | props near players |
| 3 | **The dinner bell** | At 18:30 a bell rings from the farm, heard 2.5 km off, every evening | sound and a line |
| 4 | **Lanterns on the table** | From dusk the long table outside is lit, seen from far | props near players |
| 5 | **The farm dressed** | The long table set for too many, a name card at the empty chair, jars on a cupboard, a shoe cabinet by the door, chimney smoke in the evening | props near players |
| 6 | **Their own looks** | Sunday clothes: suits, collars, a flat cap. All men | the bodies |
| 7 | **The host at the market** | Now and then the host stands by a Haven safehouse or a Red Hand fence selling jars of preserves | a body near players |
| 8 | **The boy at the well** | In towns near the farm, the boy asks travellers if they're hungry: "Father sets a place for everyone." | a body near players |
| 9 | **A voice at supper** | On the open channel at supper: grace, and a name, the last one who never arrived | radio |
| 10 | **The jars spread** | Their jars turn up in loot near the farm. Halcyon can test one: the result starts the questline | items |
| 11 | **The dead go missing** | The dead near the farm are gone by morning, with drag marks toward it | tells |
| 12 | **Their door mark** | A plate and a fork, scratched small on the door of someone lone they've asked to supper | door marks |
| 13 | **The injured man at dusk** | One of them lies hurt on a quiet road at dusk, and asks to be helped home | a body near players |
| 14 | **Wanderers talk** | "The farm out past ... feeds anyone." | lines |
| 15 | **Hunger shows** | The hungrier they are, the more of everything: more signs, the bell later, the boy further out, more of the dead taken | scales the rest |
| 16 | **The harvest supper** | Once a season, a big supper. Bell, signs and voice invite everyone near. Players who eat that night may work out what they're eating | an evening |

## Rules it keeps

- They take nobody new because of any of this: the takings stay capped as built.
- Nothing is overpowered: a jar is food, a sale is small, the injured man asks and never forces.
- People say it (MISSION_RULES rule 29). Everyone met is a man.
- Every prop is near players only, fitted with its measured size.

## Order of work

1. The every-season chance, the bell, the voice, the wanderers' line, hunger as a scale.
2. The farm dressed, the lanterns, the looks.
3. Signs at junctions, the door mark, the dead go missing.
4. The host at the market, the boy, the injured man, the jars and Halcyon's test.
5. The harvest supper.
