# The hunt: a named one

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14.

The review's gaps: few memorable fights, and no personal goal you can chase across the map. The
infected's tribes already have leaders with names and histories (INFECTED.md, "Who they are",
"Kill sites, the vigil and the alpha"). The hunt makes one of them **someone you go after**.

Player text never says "alpha" (INFECTED.md, decision 2): it's *the big one*, *the one that leads
them*, or the name the Haven gave him.

## How it starts

A faction asks, by its own reason, through a leader or the dealer, once a tribe's leader has a name:

| Faction | Why | Pays for |
|---|---|---|
| The Haven | *"That was Josef from the mill. Bring his name home."* | the leader's tag brought to the safehouse: Favor, and the name on the List |
| Bastion | the tribe sets out on the shift change; a soldier's tribe is a security matter | the kill, confirmed: Fame, and the tag for the roster |
| The Red Hand | a trophy, and the tribe's been eating their toll | the tag: tins, or a line off your debt in the book |
| Halcyon | they want him alive, or his samples | taken alive (Capture the Index's rules) or his samples: the most research |

One faction's hunt at a time per tribe; taking it from one tells the others.

## How it plays

1. **Find the trail** (one objective): his tribe's tracks (INFECTED.md, "Tracks"), its kill sites,
   a scout's post. Anyone can read a fresh kill site; a Hunter reads tracks.
2. **Learn him** (one objective): who he was gives his echo (the shift change, the long way home,
   roads). Each echo read is a line in the notebook and narrows where he'll be.
3. **Choose the time** (the player's call): by day at the lair (he lies up with his guard), at dusk
   on the move, or at a kill site's vigil.
4. **The fight**: at his lair, the tribe's own rules (the guard 8–15 m out, the young first). He's
   hurt, he runs, he heals 0.1 an hour; it can take two tries.
5. **After**: his **tag** (an heirloom, [HEIRLOOMS.md](HEIRLOOMS.md)), the vigil and the succession
   as built; the season record names who brought him down.

## Caps

His samples are the tribe's built caps (3 a season a tribe). Pay through each faction's caps. A
hunt lasts 3 season days; then the faction stops asking.

## Order of work

1. The hunt as a contract on an existing tribe leader, its two objectives, the fight at the lair.
2. The four factions' reasons and ends; the tag as an heirloom.

## Settled with the author (2026-11-14)

Whether a hunted leader knows he's hunted depends on how the hunt goes, not on a switch:

| What the hunters do | He learns | He does |
|---|---|---|
| Follow his trail quietly (tracks, kill sites, unseen) | nothing | keeps his lair and his rounds |
| Kill his pack's bodies near his ground, or are seen by one | that he's hunted | doubles his guard at the lair for a day |
| Fight and lose, or flee from his lair | that they're weak | stays, and his pack hunts the trail back toward them that night |
| Hurt him and get away | that they're dangerous | moves his lair sooner (the tribes' move, as built, brought forward) |

A leader with a history of moving (INFECTED.md, alpha histories) moves sooner still; one that has
never moved stays.
