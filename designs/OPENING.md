# The opening: the Case

**Designed, not built** (the rest of it). Approved by the author on 2026-11-14 (hook B, the case;
the fixer is Red Hand). **Built:** beat 4's case and the main story's door: the runner, the case,
its lead and its hand-overs, Proof's door ([DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-case)). What follows is
what waits: it replaces the rest of the openings' steps today (step 1's search and the backgrounds'
step 2: [DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#openings-how-every-life-starts)), and keeps how a start is
rolled, the backgrounds and the spawn screen, the contact at the door, the walking contact, the
guidance levels and the opening's place in the tracker.

The author's ask: the start should guide you into what's happening, one reason after another, and
end by putting you on the main story. It takes the structure of a good RPG opening (a personal hook,
favours that teach, a reveal, a side you pick, a lead with a name), never its characters, places or
words.

## The shape

Five beats, 25 to 35 minutes the first time, every one in the world and every one done for a person.
Each follows [MISSION_RULES.md](../docs/MISSION_RULES.md): the thing named stands there, real doors,
hand-overs by reply, one objective per step, the next step said in words.

| # | Beat | What happens | It teaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The door** | The contact talks first, and his first lines ask who you were ("You're steady with that bag. Nurse?"). Your answers settle a small habit (below) and his problem, which fits your background | the talk screen, the tracker, replies |
| 2 | **The favour** | Fetch one named thing, for him, from a place that makes sense. Your background opens a second way through. He walks with you and talks as things come up | searching, stealth, door marks, the infected, your background's own skill |
| 3 | **The line** | Favour done, he wants out. You go with him toward the line. The loudspeakers, then a drone. Ahead, a man with a pack runs for it and is shot by snipers nobody sees, at a distance. Then quiet | the perimeter, the season's premise: nobody gets out |
| 4 | **The case** | Built ([DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-case)): today the runner is already down when you get there; once beat 3 is built he falls in front of you | the main story's thing, the factions by name |
| 5 | **The fixer comes to town** | Back in town, a Red Hand crew is asking who picked it up. You pick a side, several ways (below) | sides, standing, grey choices, the town's people |

Then the lead to the station and the main story: built ([DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-case)).

## Beat 1: who you were

Your background is still picked on the spawn screen (its loadout, perk and flaw). The contact's first
talk asks about it in passing, and one reply of three gives a small **habit** for this life:

| Habit | Effect (small, capped, recovers like every effect) |
|---|---|
| Steady hands | Holds at work sites 10% shorter |
| Light feet | 10% quieter while crouched |
| A good liar | One persuade per conversation one step easier |

The habit is a ReforgerZ effect, not a perk tree, and says so on the Standing page. A veteran's door
asks nothing: the habit is picked from their last life's.

## Beat 2: the favour, by background

Every favour is one named thing, fetched for him, with a second way through for your background
(a check or tag in the dialogue engine, [DIALOGUE.md](../docs/DIALOGUE.md)). The fetch place obeys
[PLACES.md](../docs/PLACES.md#a-place-is-what-its-words-say).

| Background | His problem | The thing, and where | Your way through |
|---|---|---|---|
| Nurse | His son has a fever | the boy's medicine, from the chemist's (else the old aid post) | **[Nurse]** "That's not a fever. Hold him still": treat him on the spot, no fetch |
| Doctor | The same | the same | **[Doctor]** the same, and you name what it is |
| Mechanic | His car won't start | a battery, from the garage | **[Mechanic]** the loom: hotwire it (the car screen), no battery |
| Hunter | Something's been at his hens | his rifle's last box, from his own shed | **[Hunter]** read the tracks: it's a man, not a fox |
| Police Officer | His neighbour won't answer | the neighbour's spare key, from under the mat | **[Police]** "Open up, it's the police": the door opens from inside |
| Ex-Con | The same | the same | **[Ex-Con]** the back door's lock, quietly |
| Lab Technician | His daughter's insulin is warm (an absent child) | the cool box from the school's lab | **[Lab]** keep the insulin cold with what's at hand |
| Radio Operator | He can't raise his brother | a battery for his set, from the shop | **[Radio]** you raise the brother on your own set |
| Soldier | A lad's taken his rifle and gone | the rifle, from the lad's house | **[Soldier]** "Give it here, son": the lad hands it over |
| Raider | He owes the crews a tin | a tin, from the house next door | **[Raider]** you know the collector: it's forgotten |
| Elder | Two families want the same well | the well rota, from the church board | **[Elder]** you settle it in the street |
| Unemployed, Drifter | His dog's gone into the old shop | the dog, from the shop's back room | anyone: whistle |

The contact walks with you (the walking contact, as built) and says one line at each thing worth
knowing: a door mark ("A cross means the dead are inside. A circle, someone's still home"), an
infected ("They hear you first. Keep low"), a locked door, the broadcast from a car radio.

## Beat 3: the line

- **Where:** the opening's start is rolled with a viewpoint on the line in reach: a spot 150 to 250 m
  outside the perimeter's warning zone, on a road, 600 m to 2 km from the start (added to the start's
  place rules; a start with none falls back to beat 3's short form below).
- **The scene:** the contact stops at the viewpoint. The loudspeakers start; a drone passes. A runner
  (a spawned body with a pack) breaks from cover 100 m ahead and runs at the line; the perimeter's
  unseen snipers kill him in the warning zone. The player is never the target and never inside the
  zone; the scene ends if the player walks toward the line (the perimeter's own warnings as built).
- **The line:** the contact, after a while: *"Eleven days. They said aid was coming."*
- **Short form** (no viewpoint, a veteran, or a crowded server): the shot is heard, the runner is
  found already down at the edge of town, and the broadcast says the rest.

## Beat 5: the fixer

A Red Hand crew of three comes into the start town 2 to 4 minutes after you return, led by
**Ferda Kroupa**, who collects for the book. Polite, patient, never in a hurry. He knows the runner
was carrying something, and that someone from this street picked it up. He asks the contact first.

| Way | How | Standing (small, through the caps) |
|---|---|---|
| **Hand it over** | He pays well (tins, a favour in the book). The street thinks less of you | Red Hand +, Haven − |
| **Talk him off** | A check on your background or the liar habit: *"Bastion's patrol is two streets away."* He goes, for now | none; he remembers |
| **Rally the street** | Three neighbours each need one thing first: a door boarded (a real door), a rifle cleaned (a work site), a lookout posted (a watch). Then you stand together and he backs off; if anyone fires, it's a fight | Haven +, Red Hand − |
| **Slip away** | Out the back with the case. The street takes his visit | Haven −; he remembers |
| **Sell it, then warn the street** | Take his tins, then tell the street he's coming back for more | Red Hand + then −, Haven ± |

Every way is a reply, a work site or a fight in the world, never a menu choice. The radio mentions
what you did once, that evening.

**Selling to him.** "Hand it over" is the built sale (a Red Hand person buys it today, [DESIGN.md](../docs/DESIGN.md#the-case)) moved to Ferda: Get It Back then takes it from his lock-up.

**Ferda afterwards.** He's the opening's thread into the season: a note on your door, his men at a
toll asking after you, his voice once on the crews' channel. He wants the case, or what it was worth.
He never hunts a player (no spawned squad follows you); he turns up where the Red Hand already is.

## Shorter for those who know it

| Level | The opening |
|---|---|
| 1, learning | All five beats |
| 2, familiar | The door (no questions), the favour, the line's short form, the case, Ferda as one visit |
| 3, veteran | The door, the line's short form, the case, Ferda's note on the door; about 5 minutes |

## Many players

Each life's opening is its own: its own contact, favour, runner and case, near that player only.
Ferda's crew comes for one player at a time; two players' openings in one town share his visit.
Cases are capped like every sample (one live case per player per season counts as tissue).

## What it uses

The openings' engine and the case as built, the walking contact, work sites and carrying, the dialogue engine's checks and
tags, the perimeter as built, the Red Hand's crews and the book, reputation's caps, the majors' Proof
and its participation by life, the finale's live tissue, the tracker.

## Order of work

1. Built: the case and the lead, the item and its tag, the station's reply, Proof's door (The Date
   on the Tag, Get It Back, The Case at the Post).
2. Beats 1 and 2: the questions, the habits, the favours by background with their ways through.
3. Beat 3: the viewpoint in the start's rules, the runner, the short form.
4. Beat 5: Ferda's visit and its five ways; his later turns.
5. The shorter forms and the server checks (a fresh life reaches the case; each favour's thing is
   there; each way through Ferda ends). The case's own check is built (`RZT_Case.c`).
