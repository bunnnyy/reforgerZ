# Tonight: three things near you

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14.

The review's gap: a player with an hour comes round or logs in and sees systems, not a goal.
**Tonight** is three things worth doing near you right now, so the first minute has a direction.

## How it plays

- **When:** at every respawn ([PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md)) and every join, after the
  opening is done; and on demand in the notebook.
- **Where it shows:** one notice card (three short lines, readable on a TV), dismissed with the
  pad's back button, and a **Tonight** page in the notebook that keeps the three till they change.
- **Picking one** (a row in the notebook, A on a pad) tracks it, as taking a job does.

## What it picks from

Three, each from a different kind, within 1.5 km (walking reach: PLACES.md, "Offer reach"), never
something your standing shuts you out of:

| Kind | From | Line |
|---|---|---|
| Work | a giver with work for you, the dealer, a duty open at your camp | *"Pavel at the mill has work. He looked like he meant it."* |
| Something happening | a supply truck due, a world event, a tribe's pack restless, a toll changing hands | *"A truck's due at the north post at nine."* |
| Something to find | a landmark not yet found ([LANDMARKS.md](LANDMARKS.md)), an heirloom's rumour ([HEIRLOOMS.md](HEIRLOOMS.md)), a hunt ([ALPHA_HUNT.md](ALPHA_HUNT.md)) | *"They say the dam's keeper never left."* |
| Yours | a job you hold that's near, a companion's errand ([COMPANION.md](COMPANION.md)) | used when there's no work line |

Every line is a true thing that's there now (MISSION_RULES.md): the list reads the systems, it never
makes anything up or spawns anything.

## Order of work

1. The picker over what already exists (givers, events, trucks, tribes, held jobs); the card and the
   notebook page; picking tracks.
2. The find lines as landmarks and heirlooms land.
