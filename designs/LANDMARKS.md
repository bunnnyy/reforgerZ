# Landmarks: places worth the walk

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14.

The review's gap: most places are laid out by code from settlements, so each feels generic, and
the empty map gives little reason to cross it. Landmarks are **8 to 12 places per map that the
author builds by hand**, each with a story told by what's there, a puzzle, loot that fits the place
and an heirloom ([HEIRLOOMS.md](HEIRLOOMS.md)).

## The rules

- **Built in Game Master with the site builder** (SITES.md, "Hand-built sites"): the site menu gets
  a second list, **Landmarks**, next to the camps. A landmark is a hand-built site with no faction:
  its objects, its role spots for the few people it has, and new spot types for what a landmark needs
  (a scene, a puzzle's pieces, a loot spot, an heirloom's spot).
- **The one exception to "any map"** (CLAUDE.md): a landmark lives in the map's own coordinates.
  A map with none plays exactly as today; code never depends on one.
- **Spawned near players only**, like a hand-built camp, at the fast fill (SITES.md, "How fast a
  place fills").
- **Each landmark has, in its save:**

| Part | What | Uses |
|---|---|---|
| A story | 2 to 4 scenes in layers (TRACES.md, "Scenes"): what happened here in the first days, read by walking through | Traces' scenes and their placer |
| A puzzle | one of the built puzzles set in the place (a time-lock safe, a fuse board, a numbers station, a rat's trail) | the puzzle missions |
| Loot that fits | its own loot table by kind (a hospital: medical; a quarry: tools and explosives' empty boxes; an airfield: flight kit), at loot spots the author places, at the house's odds (no extra) | loot tables, extra finds |
| An heirloom | one heirloom's trail ends here | HEIRLOOMS.md |
| A rumour | a line people say about it, so a player hears of it before they find it | wanderers' gossip, the radio |
| A danger | one: a nest, a crawler town, a pack's lair near, a trap, the line | the infected, traps |

- **Discovery.** The first time a player comes within 50 m, the notebook gets a page for it: the
  rumour, what they saw, and a line pointing on to the next one (*"The man at the quarry said the
  dam's keeper never left."*). A full set of a map's landmarks is a Legacy point once a season.

## Candidates on Everon (the author picks in Game Master)

By kind, for the author to choose where they suit: **an airfield**, **a harbour or a quay**,
**a quarry**, **a dam or a weir if the map has one**, **the biggest church**, **a hospital or a big
clinic**, **a radio mast on a hill**, **a sawmill or a factory**, **a castle or ruin on a hill**,
**a farm estate**, **a military base outside the factions' holdings**, **a lighthouse or a lonely
coast house**. Arland second: its airfield, its base, a farm, a hill.

## What a landmark isn't

Not a faction's camp (no quartermaster, no duties), not a safe place to come round
([PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md)), not a supply drop or anything from outside.

## Order of work

1. The site builder's Landmarks list, the new spot types, the save's landmark parts.
2. One landmark built by the author on Everon end to end (story, puzzle, loot, heirloom, rumour,
   danger), played, then the rest.
3. The notebook's discovery pages and the set's Legacy point.
