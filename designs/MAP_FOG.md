# The map's fog: what's left

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14. The fog itself is built
([UI.md](../docs/UI.md#the-maps-fog)); these parts wait.

| Part | What |
|---|---|
| **Selling a survey** | A town whose ground you've seen in full can be sold as a survey to the Haven or Bastion for Favor: once per town per character, capped like every Favor (REPUTATION.md), a reply at their quartermaster |
| **Sharing maps** | Two players copy their fog onto each other: a reply on the talk screen when one talks to the other (both agree), or a paper map marked by one and handed to the other (its holder learns what the marker had seen when it was marked) |
| **A notebook page on a first visit** | Entering a place whose square you've never seen (a town, a landmark, a camp) writes a short page in the notebook that points onward to one more place by rumour |
| **The roads, not a line** | Asking about the roads and a found map follow the real roads from the town and to the job (the road network), not a strip and a disc |

## Order of work

3. Sharing maps, then selling a survey.
