## Seeds and vegetables for farming

When farming's first step is built: a farm's and a shop's table gets seed packets, and a farm's gets
the vegetables themselves. Reforger and the stack have **no seed items** and no tomatoes,
cucumbers, bell peppers or pumpkins: four seed packets and four vegetables are ours to make (eight
small prefabs), as the crafting items were. The vegetables are food (the shared food group), the
seed packets gear. DayZ's Farm loot has the same set (tomato, pepper, pumpkin, zucchini seeds, about
30 each on the map), which is a good sign.
