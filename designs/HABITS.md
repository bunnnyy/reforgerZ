# Habits: who you become

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14. It grows the three habits of
[OPENING.md](OPENING.md) (beat 1) into a small set earned by doing, and is written for
[PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md): a character keeps them until reset.

The review's gap: character growth is thin; reputation was the only thing you built, and it reset at
death. Habits are **what your hands learned**: small, capped, earned only by doing the thing,
shown on the Standing page.

## The habits

| Habit | Earned by | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Steady hands | 20 work-site holds finished | holds 10% shorter |
| Light feet | 15 infected killed unseen (stealth's Hidden or Low at the kill) | 10% quieter crouched |
| A good liar | 6 persuades or lies that worked in talk | one persuade a conversation one step easier |
| Field dresser | 10 bandages, dressings or treatments on others | treating others 15% quicker |
| Iron stomach | 20 meals eaten from a camp's pot or a fire you lit | food and water last 10% longer |
| Road sense | 10 km walked at night | the map's road you're on names where it goes |
| Known face | 15 errands or duties done for one faction | that faction's people greet you by name, and one price there is 5% less |
| Cold nerve | 5 times you held still Hidden with infected within 15 m | your stealth bar fills 10% slower |

- **The opening** gives one of the first three at the door, as OPENING.md says; it counts as earned.
- **At most three habits** at a time. Earning a fourth asks which to let go (a reply on the Standing
  page, with a pad). Every number is a cap, never stacked with a perk of the same kind past +15%.
- **They fade if you stop.** A habit not used in 5 real days of play fades (Standing page: *"Your
  hands have forgotten."*) and is earned again at half the count.
- **Kept across deaths**, lost at a reset (PERSISTENCE.md).

## Shown

The Standing page lists your habits with one line each and the count toward the next (*"Steady
hands: 14 of 20 holds."*). A habit earned is a notice and a line from whoever's nearest.
