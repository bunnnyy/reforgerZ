# The locker: what you can't bear to lose

**Designed, not built.** Approved by the author on 2026-11-14. Written for
[PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md): a character lasts, but a death loses everything carried.

## What it is

A **locker with your name on it** at one camp, holding **3 of your own items** across deaths
until you reset or the season wipes. It's the one place loot outlives a death.

## How it plays

- **Where:** at a camp you're **Known or better** with: a Haven safehouse, a holding, Halcyon's lab.
  You pick one camp; moving it to another costs 10 Favor with the new one and empties nothing (the
  items move as data).
- **What it is in the world:** the camp's own storage (a footlocker or crate, a hand-built site's
  stores spot), opened with **vanilla's storage action**, showing only your three slots. Default first:
  vanilla's inventory, no new screen.
- **What can go in:** your own items: anything you carried in, three of them. Not an heirloom
  ([HEIRLOOMS.md](HEIRLOOMS.md)), not a quest item (they come round with you anyway), not a
  faction's lent kit.
- **Kept as data:** the prefab, its ammo and its condition, saved with the profile. The props exist
  only while you have it open.
- **Lost with the camp:** if you fall to Watched or worse with the camp's faction, the locker is
  shut to you; it opens again when you're back to Known. A Red Hand locker costs 2 Favor a season
  day, written in the book; unpaid for 3 days, they sell what's in it.

## Caps

Three items, one locker, per character. No item doubles, nothing is made. A death doesn't touch it.

## Order of work

1. The data store and the three slots, opened with vanilla's storage at a camp.
2. Choosing and moving the camp; shut when you fall; the Red Hand's rent.
