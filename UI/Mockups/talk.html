Step 1. When they speak, only their line shows, in the band in the lower middle; their name and sub-line stay top right in their faction colour. The thin bar fills over the reading time (0.05 s a character, 2.5 s to 9 s), then the replies come by themselves. A, Space or a click skips to them; the right stick or the wheel scrolls a long line.
Step 2. The band becomes the reply box, outlined in the faction colour, with only your choices: the selected one filled and outlined, check tags in the faction colour, a reply you can't take faint with what it needs, one with nothing new behind it grey. The arrows at the left show there's another page (down here, on page 1), "1/2" bottom right; LB/RB, Q/E or a click on an arrow turn it.
Step 3. The reply you say fades and their answer takes the band: step 1 again. B or Esc leaves from either step. Nothing dims the world, and the person stays in view.
