# The world-building kit (docs/SITES.md, "The world-building kit"): what the prop mods and the base game give for
# dressing sites, Traces scenes, mission places and the map itself. Read by people and by scripts; build.py doesn't touch it.
#
# One line each, fields split by " | ":
#   ResourceName | kind | from | suits | size | what it is | notes
#
# kind   prop: one object, also in props.txt, so pieces.py can use it in a piece (build.py).
#        composition: a group to place whole (Workbench, or RZ_Util.SpawnPrefab from a script).
#        building: a building or a big ruin, to place by hand in Workbench.
# from   the mod it needs loaded (addon.gproj has them all), or the base game.
# suits  towns (abandoned towns), checkpoints (checkpoints and the perimeter), haven, bastion, redhand, halcyon (the lab),
#        graves (graves and the dead), roads, farms, wilds, coast, indoors.
# size   a prop: width x depth x height in metres (its measured box). A group: its box, or r<N>, its parts' reach from the middle.
# notes  what the dedicated server logged when it spawned the piece, and what in it a player can use. "-" is nothing to say.
#
# Every line was spawned once on the dedicated server (game 1.9) with the mods loaded. Nothing here was seen in play
# unless its notes say so: look at a piece in Workbench before building on it.
